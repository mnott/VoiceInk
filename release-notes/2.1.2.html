What's New in VoiceInk 2.1.2

Settings backups now carry everything, so moving to another Mac no longer loses your setup.

  • 💾 Export/Import covers the pinned-destination settings — Per-application delivery rules, the pin shortcut, and the tint colour and its on/off switch are now included in a settings backup. Previously they were silently dropped, so importing on a second machine left you to configure them again by hand.
  • 💾 Two more settings included — "Auto Enter after transcription" and "Append trailing space" are backed up as well; neither was before.
  • 🔒 Older backups still import — Every added field is optional, and a backup that predates them leaves those settings untouched rather than resetting them to defaults.
