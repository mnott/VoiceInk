What's New in VoiceInk 2.2.0

Meeting Capture: record a meeting with both your microphone and the meeting's audio, and get a speaker-labelled transcript while it runs — plus a safer History.

  • 🎙️ Meeting Capture with automated speaker identification — Captures your microphone and the meeting's system audio as two channels, diarizes the remote participants live, and delivers labelled transcript chunks to your pinned destination while the meeting runs. Stop the capture and a complete speaker-labelled meeting note lands in History with the meeting's start time.
  • 🧑‍💼 Speaker library — Voices get stable ids and names (Audio → Speakers). Label a voice once and every later meeting shows the name instead of an anonymous speaker id. "Identify Speakers" on a History entry re-diarizes an existing note, and "Name Speaker" during a capture registers whoever is talking. In-person mode diarizes everyone sharing your Mac's microphone.
  • 🔇 Silence calibration for noisy places — A train or a busy office can sit above the voice detector's silence threshold, so your pauses never register and nothing gets sent. "Calibrate Silence" (menu bar → Meeting, or its own shortcut) measures the next two seconds of ambience and pins it as the session's noise floor; pauses split correctly from then on. A length-capped chunk always sends something instead of silently deferring forever.
  • 📋 Meeting menu in the menu bar — Meeting Capture, Send Meeting Chunk, Calibrate Silence and Name Speaker are all in the menu bar with their shortcuts shown next to them, so the hotkeys are one glance away.
  • 🗑️ Recently Deleted for History — Deleted transcriptions (and their audio) are kept for 30 days and can be restored. Delete-key and multi-select (shift-click, cmd-click, cmd-A) work in History, with proper plurals.
  • 🛡️ Sturdier captures — Echo of the meeting's own audio no longer steals your words into remote-speaker turns; short interjections get their own turns; stopping mid-sentence keeps the speaker label on the final chunk; chunk text is sanitized so a line can never start with / ! # (dictation and chat commands stay safe); and meeting files dropped on the app re-transcribe with Me/Others labelling.
